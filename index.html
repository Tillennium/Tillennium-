    Institutional intelligence mapped across six core operational layers

    Tillennium fuses regulatory filings, real-time supply chain telemetry, structural valuation models, and stress tests into a single, verifiable research framework designed for institutional execution.

    01 / SUPPLY CHAIN

    Supply chain arbitrage

    Companies disclose their dependencies slowly and unevenly. Tillenium maps customers, suppliers and logistics lanes across tiers, then compares what physical-world signals imply with what filings assume. The arbitrage is the gap between the two: a supplier lead time stretching or a port backing up shows in operations before it shows in guidance.

    • Tier 1 to Tier 3 supplier and customer mapping
    • Single-source and concentration flags
    • Lane, port and lead-time drift overlays
    • Gaps ranked by how far reality has moved from disclosure
    02 / SEC EDGAR

    SEC EDGAR telemetry

    Every 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 13F and Form 4 is parsed as it is filed and treated as a signal stream. Tillenium tracks how language, numbers and commitments change from one filing to the next, not only what a single filing says.

    • Filing-over-filing changes in risk factors and MD&A
    • Customer concentration, purchase obligations and inventory movement
    • 8-K events classified by materiality
    • Institutional (13F) and insider (Form 4) position changes
    03 / RISK

    Risk assessment

    Each exposure is scored on probability and impact, then rolled into a composite risk score with its drivers itemized. Predictive models add scenario bands, and the assumptions behind them are written beside every result so a committee can challenge the inputs.

    • Probability against impact matrix per exposure
    • Composite score with drivers listed
    • 90-day scenario bands with stated assumptions
    • Alerts when a score moves materially
    04 / DOSSIER

    Institutional dossier

    The output is a structured, multi-page PDF written for an investment committee: thesis, evidence, risks and scenarios, with a source map where every claim traces back to an EDGAR filing or data feed. It is ready to circulate, not a raw data dump.

    • Executive summary with thesis and key findings
    • Catalyst timeline linked to the filings that triggered it
    • Supply chain and risk exhibits
    • Appendix source map for audit and compliance
    05 / INTELLIGENCE

    Company intelligence

    Comprehensive equity profiling and fundamental data aggregation parsed automatically from primary regulatory channels. Tracks corporate structures, governance shifts, and historical performance metrics to maintain deep visibility into target firms.

    • Automated peer group comparisons and executive compensation tracking
    • Board structure mapping and governance signal monitoring
    • Corporate restructuring and asset divestiture flags
    • Real-time fundamental metrics sync across filings
    06 / ANALYSIS

    Portfolio analysis

    Systematic allocation tracking, factor exposure decomposition, and tracking error attribution designed for rigorous risk management. Monitors multi-asset correlation matrices and generates executive-ready performance telemetry exhibits.

    • Factor exposure decomposition and tracking error attribution
    • Automated rebalancing signals based on quantitative model outputs
    • Multi-asset correlation matrix and concentration monitoring
    • Instant generation of portfolio performance telemetry exhibits
